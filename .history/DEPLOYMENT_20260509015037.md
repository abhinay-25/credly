# 🚀 Credly Badge - Deployment Guide

## Quick Deploy to Netlify (FREE)

### Step 1: Sign Up
- Go to [netlify.com](https://netlify.com)
- Click "Sign up" → Choose GitHub or Email
- Complete signup (1 minute)

### Step 2: Deploy
- Click "Add new site" → "Deploy manually"
- **Drag and drop this entire CREADLY folder** onto Netlify
- Wait for upload (10-30 seconds)
- ✅ Your site is LIVE!

### Step 3: Get Your URL
- Netlify gives you a free URL: `https://xxxxx.netlify.app`
- Share this URL with anyone!

---

## 📱 How to Use Your Deployed Site

1. Visit your Netlify URL
2. **Enter your name** in the form
3. Click **"View Badge"** button
4. Your badge displays with your name
5. Click **"Verified"** to see verification details
6. Click **"Celebrate"** for celebration icon

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Is it really free?**  
A: Yes! Netlify's free plan includes unlimited sites and 100GB/month bandwidth.

**Q: Can I use a custom domain?**  
A: Yes, but that's a paid add-on ($12/year for a domain).

**Q: How do I update my site after deploying?**  
A: Update files locally, then drag-and-drop the folder again on Netlify.

**Q: Does localStorage work on Netlify?**  
A: Yes! Your name is saved locally in the browser.
